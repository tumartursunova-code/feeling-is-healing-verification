About Us

Feeling is Healing is a youth-led initiative exploring emotional intelligence, adolescent wellbeing, self-awareness, and digital mental health through research, reflection, and interactive experiences.

We aim to create meaningful spaces where teenagers feel understood — not just informed — in an increasingly overwhelming digital world.

emotional intelligence adolescent wellbeing research safe digital design self-awareness

Youth-Centered Approach

Our work is built around the real emotional experiences of teenagers rather than generic mental health advice.

Research & Reflection

We explore emotional patterns, digital wellbeing, self-perception, and adolescent experiences through structured participation and reflection.

Future Vision

Feeling is Healing aims to evolve into a broader ecosystem of emotionally intelligent digital experiences, including interactive tools and community platforms.

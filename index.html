About Us

Feeling is Healing is a youth-led initiative focused on mental health, self-awareness, and adolescent wellbeing. We aim to create meaningful and mentally safe experiences that help teenagers better understand themselves in an increasingly overwhelming digital world.

In the future, we are building a broader digital platform that may include interactive tools and a mobile application designed to support reflection, emotional awareness, and everyday wellbeing practices for teenagers.

If this vision resonates with you, you are welcome to join us and be part of this growing initiative.
